About

Meaghan Janedis

Meaghan is a seasoned startup CEO with a long history of helping people achieve their goals. She started her career on Wall Street in various sales roles, where she developed and built relationships with many of her firm's key clients.

After leaving Wall Street to start her family, she went on to found multiple companies in different industries where she chased her entrepreneurial spirit which she gleaned from her parents. The companies align well with her passions including parenthood, athletics, eldercare and above all, ethics. The absolute core of everything Meaghan does must be ethically sound.

Eagle Point Coaching pulls together all of Meaghan's rich experience to a more personal level to help people achieve their professional and personal aspirations.

Meaghan earned her B.S.B.A. from Rosemont College and worked in Financial Services at Bear, Stearns, and Merrill Lynch where she was appropriately licensed in Series 7 and Series 63, and then later at New York Life SIE, Series 6, 7 and 63.

Meaghan captained her college Tennis Team to the 1992, 1993 NCAA Div. III Championship Title.

She and her husband, John, of 24 years are the Proud Sponsors of many New York and Long Island Institutions, including, but not limited to: Buckley Country Day School, Christ Church, Manhasset, Elizabeth Seton Children's Center, The Greek Orthodox Church of the Holy Resurrection, The Green Vale School, Episcopal High School in Alexandria, VA, Mt. Sinai Hospital, Nassau County Police and Fire Departments, New York Presbyterian / Weill Cornell Medical Center, Northwell Health Systems, Ryan's Case for Smiles (formerly ConKerr Cancer), and St. Vincent's Hospital, Westchester.

And perhaps most importantly, she personally mentors a few young adults to help each of them launch their educational and professional careers.

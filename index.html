Who Is Dr. Brianna?

Dr. Brianna Olamiju is a board-certified dermatologist with expertise in both medical and cosmetic dermatology. A native New Yorker, she completed her undergraduate studies at Columbia University, earned her medical degree from the Yale School of Medicine, and completed her dermatology residency at Weill Cornell New York-Presbyterian Hospital. Dr. Olamiju has published numerous journal articles and textbook chapters, and her work has been recognized by the American Academy of Dermatology and JAMA Dermatology. She serves on the Board of Directors of the Women's Dermatologic Society and is an active member of the American Society for Dermatologic Surgery and the Skin of Color Society. Her clinical interests span acne, hair loss, eczema, psoriasis, pigmentary disorders, and skin cancer screening, alongside a full suite of cosmetic treatments including filler, lasers, and microneedling.

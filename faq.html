Frequently Asked Questions

What is executive coaching?

Executive coaching is a professional development tool that helps leaders improve their performance and grow, which can lead to increased organizational efficiency and productivity. It involves one-on-one sessions between a coach and a leader, or team, where the coach helps the leader identify goals, work through challenges, and build the skills and confidence needed to perform at their best.

What are the benefits of executive coaching?

Executive coaching can have many benefits for individuals and organizations, including personal growth, leadership development, better work-life balance, and fresh perspective on challenges you’re facing.

How long does an executive coaching session last?

Executive coaching sessions can vary in length and frequency, but typically last an hour and recur weekly. The length and frequency of sessions can vary depending on the needs of the client, so we suggest beginning by scheduling an initial session.
